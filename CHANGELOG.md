# Changelog

## 2.0.13 — 2026-10-25 — Minecraft 1.21.1

Clean-up of the content added in 2.0.12: the guide book is now **Recetas De La Buela** and every internal name, folder and id was renamed.

### ⚠️ Breaking
- The ids of the seven food effects changed (they now live under `ultimatefoods:fisura/<name>`) and the guide book got a new id. Active effects from 2.0.12 are dropped, and a 2.0.12 guide book has to be crafted again.

### ⚔️ Changed
- New guide book name and cover.
- Internal packages, folders and class names were renamed; recipes moved to a new folder.

## 2.0.12 — 2026-10-25 — Minecraft 1.21.1

Criollo content: the mate ritual, the grill, the Essen pot, 36 Criollo foods and drinks, a guide book and a few easter eggs. Everything lives in the new **"Artinna Re Fisura"** creative tab.

### ✨ Added
- **Mate**: yerba mate tree (leaves, sapling, log and green planks), dried and ground yerba, Verdeflor packages, the yerbera and the sugar pouch (they store portions), the mate in six states, the kettle (fill it, heat it), the bombilla and yuyos. Brew it, drink it (4 sips per brew) and pass it to players and villagers. Four flavors with their own effects: Tranquility, Out of my way!, Development and Prosperity.
- **Placeable mates**: mates, kettles, yerba packages and bombillas can be set down with Shift + right click; fill, flavor and brew a set-down mate, stick the bombilla in separately and unwash a washed mate with a brush.
- **The yerbatero**: a villager profession with the yerbera as job site and its own trades.
- **Criollo foods and drinks** (36): asado, chorizo, choripan, provoleta, milanesas, milanesa sandwich, guiso, locro, noquis, alfajor, medialuna, pancakes, flan, fried cake, empanadas, Pitusas, Pepas, fugazzeta, the Mega Dibu, Fernet Branca and three beers, with new effects: Full Belly, Grill Fire, Sugar Rush, Tipsy, Good Vibes, Overstuffed and Penalty Saver.
- **The grill** (cooks 6 things at once) and the **Essen pot** (guiso and locro, with its own recipe type, open lid, cooking state on click and in Jade, and a JEI category).
- **Placeable foods**: every Criollo food has a 3D model and can be set down with Shift + right click and eaten from the table; the asado is eaten in four bites.
- **Guide book** (needs Patchouli, optional): custom 3D item and GUI, one sheet per item and block, an Ultimate Foods section and an explanation of every easter egg. Craft it with a book and a yerba mate.
- **La Copa**, a golden trophy in honor of Lionel Andres Messi, and easter eggs: rename a villager "Messi", rename a pig "carpincho", the wandering trader's sales pitch, and a new face on the sun.
- Recipes for the four drinks and the flan; guiso accepts dough instead of rice or pasta.

### ⚔️ Changed
- Foods, recipes, models and languages of the Criollo content are generated by datagen (en_us, es_es, es_ar, es_mx).
- Patchouli joins JEI and Jade as an optional integration.

### 🐛 Fixed
- The bombilla leans toward the player, and the trader's audio no longer overlaps itself.

## [2.0.10] — 2026-10-10 — Minecraft 1.21.1

Ultimate Foods is now only the foods and drinks. The equipment and materials moved to **UltimateArmoury: Tools And Weapons**, and the utility items and music discs moved to **Ultimate Tweaks**.

### ⚠️ Breaking
- Everything that is not a food left this mod, together with its item and block ids. Worlds made with 2.0.9 or earlier lose those items.
- **Farmer's Delight** is now required. It is also a dependency of the raw burger meat recipe.
- AppleSkin stays required.

### ✨ Added
- The raw burger meat recipe uses any **Farmer's Delight knife** instead of the Netherite Dagger, which now belongs to UltimateArmoury.

### ⚔️ Changed
- The mod is reduced to the 14 foods, their 6 eating sounds, the Poop effect and the burger drops of the Sniffer, Armadillo, Villager and Warden.
- GeckoLib, Patchouli, Ex Deorum and AllTheModium are no longer dependencies.

### 🗑️ Removed
- Materials, ores, armor, tools, weapons, hammers, meshes, compressed blocks and the Nadienite fluid: now in UltimateArmoury.
- The 21 utility items, the 2 music discs and their sounds: now in Ultimate Tweaks.

## [2.0.9] — 2026-09-04 — Minecraft 1.21.1

### ✨ Added
- Three full armor and tool tiers: **Mushashite** → **JoanFoite** → **Nadienite**, each with Gorro, Remera, Gayumbos, Medias, sword, axe, pickaxe, shovel and hoe.
- Stepped set bonuses with per-piece tooltips: Luck and extra health on Mushashite; Water Breathing, Speed II and Piglin/Creeper immunity on JoanFoite; Night Vision, Resistance III with creative flight, Jump III, Speed III plus Enderman and Phantom perks on Nadienite.
- Upgrade recipes between tiers using Corazón De Los Caídos, Corazón De Los Fantasmas and Corazón De La Elite.
- Ores, deepslate ores, raw ores, ingots, nuggets and storage blocks for the three tiers, plus steel and netherite nuggets, an Ender Pearl block and a new Nether Star block texture.
- 3D wizard hat helmet rendered through GeckoLib.
- Patchouli guide book and JEI information pages for every item, tool and integration.
- Ex Deorum integration: 7 sieve meshes, 7 hammers and their compressed versions, sieve and compressed sieve drops, and crucible heat sources. Meshes and hammers follow the real tier order — steel above iron, the three custom tiers after netherite — and the whole chain is obtainable in skyblock.
- AllTheModium tiers layered on top of the Nadienite mesh and hammer. The unobtainium hammer is unbreakable.
- Compressed blocks 1x–9x for steel, mushashite, joanfoite, nadienite, nether star and ender pearl, with a block-count tooltip.
- `ultimatefoods-common.toml` config to disable each integration individually.

### ⚔️ Changed
- The old Nadienite armor was removed and replaced by the new three-tier progression with renamed pieces.
- The custom flight effect was dropped in favour of `neoforge:creative_flight`.
- Avaritia support is disabled.
- Data providers moved to `datagen/providers` and every registration class to `registry/`.

### 🐛 Fixes
- The Nadienite fluid is replaceable again, so blocks can be placed on top of it and it can be scooped up like water or lava.
